Building Geospatial Solutions for Environmental Challenges

I am a Geography researcher specializing in Geographic Information Systems, Remote Sensing, Earth Observation and environmental spatial modelling. My research interests focus on climate-induced hazards, cryosphere dynamics, GLOF susceptibility, disaster risk reduction and climate adaptation.

My MPhil research investigated Glacial Lake Outburst Flood susceptibility and community adaptation in Nagar Valley, Gilgit-Baltistan using multi-source satellite and geospatial datasets.

I combine GIS, satellite imagery, DEM analysis, climate datasets, spatial modelling and data analytics to transform complex environmental data into meaningful scientific insights for climate resilience and sustainable environmental management.
