I am Saba Nazir Ahmad, a Geography and Earth Sciences researcher specializing in Geographic Information Systems (GIS), Remote Sensing, Earth Observation and environmental geospatial modelling.

My academic and professional experience combines physical geography, geomorphology, climatology, environmental science, satellite image processing and spatial analytics to investigate complex environmental problems.

My MPhil research focuses on Glacial Lake Outburst Floods (GLOFs) in Nagar Valley, Pakistan, integrating Sentinel-2, Landsat, SRTM DEM and CHIRPS precipitation datasets with GIS-based spatial modelling and multi-criteria analysis.

My broader research direction is toward climate-induced hazards, flood and landslide susceptibility, cryosphere dynamics, climate adaptation and explainable machine learning for disaster risk reduction.
