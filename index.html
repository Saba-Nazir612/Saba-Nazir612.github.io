Building Geospatial Solutions for Environmental Challenges

I am a Geography researcher specializing in Geographic Information Systems, Remote Sensing, Earth Observation and environmental spatial modelling.


My research focuses on climate-induced hazards, cryosphere dynamics, Glacial Lake Outburst Floods (GLOFs), disaster risk reduction, climate adaptation and environmental change.


My MPhil research investigates GLOF susceptibility and community adaptation in Nagar Valley, Gilgit-Baltistan, using multi-source satellite and geospatial datasets.


Research Vision: To integrate Earth Observation, GIS, spatial modelling and Explainable GeoAI to better understand environmental hazards and support climate resilience, disaster risk reduction and sustainable environmental management.
