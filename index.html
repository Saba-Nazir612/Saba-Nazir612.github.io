Who I Am

About Me

Research-driven geospatial scientist working at the intersection of geography, climate and Earth observation.

Building Geospatial Solutions for Environmental Challenges

I am a Geography researcher specializing in Geographic Information Systems, Remote Sensing, Earth Observation and environmental spatial modelling.

My research interests focus on climate-induced hazards, cryosphere dynamics, Glacial Lake Outburst Floods (GLOFs), disaster risk reduction and climate adaptation.

My MPhil research investigates GLOF susceptibility and community adaptation in Nagar Valley, Gilgit-Baltistan using multi-source satellite and geospatial datasets.

I combine GIS, satellite imagery, DEM analysis, climate datasets, spatial modelling and data analytics to transform complex environmental data into meaningful scientific insights for climate resilience and sustainable environmental management.
